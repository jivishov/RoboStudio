# Circuit Lab Asset Attribution

RoboStudio Circuit Lab uses original project visuals and approved pinned source references for future Wokwi Elements extraction.

## Wokwi Elements

Wokwi Elements is licensed under the MIT License. RoboStudio pins Wokwi visual source references to:

- Repository: `wokwi/wokwi-elements`
- Version: `v1.9.2`
- Commit: `3c8178e`
- Notice: `LICENSES/wokwi-elements-MIT.txt`

No Wokwi graphic is currently bundled as production artwork. Any future adaptation must be a static SVG presentation record with upstream Lit wrappers, events, and simulator behavior removed.

## RoboStudio Originals

RoboStudio-owned visuals in this scope include the BB400-style breadboard vector, standard servo educational visual, fixed 6 V supply, 470 uF capacitor, 130-size DC motor, potentiometer, and simplified L298N procedural fallback.

These visuals are factual educational drawings and are not copied from manufacturer, marketplace, retailer, forum, or Fritzing artwork.

The photorealistic Circuit Lab wrappers are original RoboStudio top-down educational bitmap renders extracted from a generated 6 x 6 source sheet, chroma-keyed into transparent PNG component assets, and embedded into SVG wrappers sized by the local physical catalog. They are presentation layers only; terminal coordinates, connector compatibility, and wiring behavior remain defined by RoboStudio source code.

## Blocked Sources

Fritzing production graphics are not included. Their graphics remain blocked pending explicit repository-license compatibility and share-alike review.
